========================================================================
IMAGES FOLDER - PLACE YOUR MEDIA HERE
========================================================================

Add your personal and project images to this folder:

Suggested image filenames to use:
1. profile.jpg         - Your profile photo (Square ratio, e.g., 400x400px)
2. featured-project.jpg - Your flagship/featured project screenshot (1200x800px)
3. project1.jpg        - Project 1 screenshot
4. project2.jpg        - Project 2 screenshot
... up to project10.jpg
5. personal-photo.jpg  - For the Fun Facts media section
6. intro.mp4 (or video) - For the Fun Facts video player

To connect your image in any HTML file:
Replace:
  <div class="media-placeholder">...</div>
With:
  <img src="images/your-image-filename.jpg" alt="Your description">

Enjoy customizing your Girly Tech portfolio!
